# Project Summary

## Research area
Digital Twins and Cloud Computing

## Working title
Evaluating MQTT QoS and Edge Buffering Strategies for Resilient Cloud-Based Digital Twin Synchronization Under Unstable Networks

## Research problem
Cloud-based Digital Twins require timely and reliable state synchronization, but packet loss, delay, jitter, and temporary disconnections can make the cloud twin stale or inconsistent. MQTT QoS and edge buffering can improve reliability, but they may also introduce latency, bandwidth, duplicate-processing, storage, and recovery overhead. This study will experimentally quantify those trade-offs.

## Primary research question
How do MQTT Quality of Service levels and durable edge buffering strategies affect the freshness, reliability, latency, consistency, and communication overhead of cloud-based Digital Twin synchronization under unstable network conditions?

## Keywords
Digital Twin; MQTT; Edge Computing; Cloud Computing; QoS; Synchronization

## Planned contribution
A reproducible benchmark and practical guidance for choosing MQTT QoS and edge-buffering configurations according to network quality and Digital Twin freshness/reliability requirements.
