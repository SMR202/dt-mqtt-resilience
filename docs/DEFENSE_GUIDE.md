# Research defense prompts

1. Why choose a rate-monitoring paper for a freshness study? Its official MQTT device/twin path is executable and closely fits communication resilience. Its actual security results are not relabeled as synchronization results; the extension asks a separate systems question.
2. Is coalescing novel? It is established freshness-oriented practice. Our claimed contribution is its auditable combination with durable local history, fair service and monotonic application in a matched recovery experiment; broad novelty requires further mentor review.
3. Why can reliable MQTT coexist with missing samples? Samples may be intentionally suppressed, expired or still queued before submission. QoS protects particular protocol delivery exchanges, not all values generated by an application.
4. Why measure age as well as latency? Low latency of the few messages received does not prove all devices are current. AoI samples held state continuously, including intervals with no updates.
5. Does the proposal preserve complete history? Locally, all generated updates are in the audit table for final live runs. Intermediate updates are not all delivered to the live twin. Remote archival delivery was not implemented.
6. Does the experiment establish cloud or packet-loss performance? No. It uses a deployable cloud-side role co-located on loopback and application-shaped dispatch. The simulation is a separate model.
7. Why report an unbuffered baseline that performs well? It provides an honest comparison: discarding old current-state samples can preserve freshness. FIFO's advantage is retaining queued history, not universal freshness.
8. Why is there a guard ablation? Delayed application snapshots can arrive out of sequence; guarding prevents state rollback. Its measured benefit depends on load/jitter, and some conditions show no difference.
9. Why does nominal capacity not equal measured throughput? OS sleep resolution, disk commits and acknowledgement waits reduce the effective service rate. The code measures outcomes instead of assuming the configured maximum was achieved.
10. What do bootstrap intervals mean here? Paired-run exploratory variation under the chosen seeds/host. Three short live repetitions cannot establish broad industrial robustness or statistical significance across all cases.
11. What happened during reproduction? We executed the original MQTT function bodies in a native adapter and found threshold/dependency/capture discrepancies and a missing calibration-completion message. Failed and repaired evidence are retained.
12. What must happen before submission? Human review, instructor approval/feedback, official Overleaf integration, attribution and any externally required experiments. Do not claim that an AI-generated package itself proves student understanding or publishability.
